Life Skills
Workout · Recovery · Nutrition · Sports & Self defence
Workout · Recovery · Nutrition · In workout we have dynamic warmups, weightlifting, sports & self defence which are important life skills · Sports like swimming can be life saving · Self defence is also an important life skill, but avoid fights whenever possible, try communication, de escalation, or leaving first · Self defence should be the last option if things go wrong
Lifestyle
Focus on natural things / ways with a minimalist approach, educate yourself, gain diverse knowledge & use logic
Reduce modern high tech digital life, stick to essentials, remove unnecessary apps & use chrome instead, maintain a natural yet modern lifestyle
Wake at 7, freshen up, meditate, skin, oral & hair care, sleep at 11
Develop a good personality, styling, skills / hobbies, extroversion & social network
A healthy mind, environment & body are the major keys to mental health
Split
Dynamic warmup → weightlifting ( machines & free weights ) → cardio → self defence
Mon, thu - back ( 3 ), arms ( 2 ), shoulders ( 1 ) + cardio sports / mma
Tue, fri - legs ( 3 ), chest ( 2 ), abs ( 1 ) + cardio sports / mma
Wed - calisthenics ( 3 ) + self defence w tools ( 2 ) + neck ( 2 ) + long run ( 1 )
Execution
Choose a weight where the Zth rep feels very hard & close to failure, perform controlled reps with a natural full ROM, maintain proper form & never ego lift, with 1 RIR, all for injury prevention
For saftey & support, have some kind of backups & supporting gears, backups like - saftey bars, spotters etc & supporting gears like - knee sleeves, lifting straps etc, listen to your body, warm up properly & if something doesn't feel comfortable or safe, don't proceed or be cautious
Heavier weights for big muscle groups with compound movements for strength & indirect muscle use | lighter weights for small muscle groups with isolation movements for specific targeting
Reps - big muscles ( 10 ), mid muscles ( 12 ), small muscles ( 15 )
Pyramid warmup sets - 3 sets × Z reps, low weight w high reps, mid weight w mid reps & heavy weight w low reps
Same for calisthenics, where low weight is replaced by an easier exercise with longer hold times & so on
Target sets - 2 × Z reps ( weights ) | 2 × Z sec hold w micro reps ( cali )
Progressive overload gradually with 1 - 2 kg ( weights ) | 5 - 10 sec ( cali )
Now the above guidelines are for weightlifting, but what about cardio & self defence, we don't really have fixed guidelines for sports, you can either do them for as long as you want or follow a structured format like sets, for eg badminton has 3 sets of 21 points or you can do 4 sets of 21 points
Target sets - 4 × 21 pts like for badminton
Run at a slow pace with a natural stride & light mid foot landing, maintaining smooth & proper form
Easy pace run for X km with walk breaks as needed for long runs
Add +1 km to long runs when ready & keep increasing pace
Recovery
Sat & sun are recovery days, wed is active recovery
Have quality sleep & a consistent sleep routine
Morning - 6:30, lights brighten (30 min fade), nature sounds (30 min fade), sunlight
Night - 10:30, lights dim through smart lighting (30 min fade), bedtime mode on phone
Exception - sun 12:30 - 7:30 am (sat late night)
Nutrition
Natural, minimally processed whole foods with good nutrition
Have a balanced diet with protein, carbs & fiber, for maximum muscle building take protein at 1.5 g / kg bodyweight & stay hydrated
Use apps to calculate your maintenance calories & then bulk, maintain or cut accordingly

If You Are Lost
Stay calm, stop moving, find shelter
Signal rescuers — whistle, mirror, fire smoke
Stay in one place — easier to find
Conserve energy & water
Leave signs of your path if you must move
If Power Goes Out
Keep fridge closed — food lasts 4 hrs
Use torch, not candles if possible
Unplug devices to avoid surge damage
Check on elderly & vulnerable neighbours
Use power bank for phone — keep calls short
If There Is a Fire
Alert everyone — shout, pull alarm
Get low under smoke & crawl to exit
Feel doors before opening — hot = danger
Never use lifts
Once out — do not go back in
Call 101 from a safe distance
If There Is a Flood
Move to higher ground immediately
Avoid walking in moving water
Turn off electricity at the mains
Do not drive through flooded roads
Take emergency kit & documents
If There Is an Earthquake
Drop, cover, hold on
Get under sturdy table or against interior wall
Stay away from windows & heavy furniture
If outside — move away from buildings & wires
After shaking stops — check for injuries & gas leaks
